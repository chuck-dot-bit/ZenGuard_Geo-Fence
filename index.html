How TSA works

01
Geofence set
Your property boundary is mapped and programmed into the TSA platform. The guard's authorized zone is defined down to the metre.
One-time site setup
02
Live GPS tracking
Guard position is broadcast every 30 seconds. You see a live breadcrumb trail of every movement — and every pause — across the entire shift.
30-sec update interval
03
Checkpoint scans
Digital scanning stations at each checkpoint — scanned by the guard, logged with GPS coordinates, timestamp, and optional photo. No paper.
Every round, verified
04
Idle alert fires
If a guard is stationary beyond the timed patrol window, management receives an immediate push notification. No exceptions, no delays.
Instant escalation
05
Digital DAR
At end of shift, a full Digital Activity Report is auto-generated — every checkpoint, every incident, every patrol time — delivered before the next shift starts.
No paper, no delays
06
Your dashboard
Everything lands in your live dashboard — accessible from any device, anywhere, any time. One view of your entire security operation, in real time.
